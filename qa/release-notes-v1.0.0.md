# Sadman's Parable v1.0.0

**Your story is ready. Unfortunately, you weren't consulted.**

An original, first-person psychological fable. A building approved your life at nine seventeen this morning, before you had done anything — and the Department of Almost keeps a file on you that is older than you are.

## Play

- **In your browser:** <https://mdsadman2004.github.io/sadmans-parable/>
- **Offline:** download `Sadmans-Parable-Playable.zip` below, extract it, and double-click `PLAY.bat` (or just open `Sadman's Parable.html`).

`Sadman's Parable.html` is the entire game. No install, no server, no account, and no network access at runtime.

## What is in it

- **16 explorable spaces and 12 endings**, from a mundane office to suspended archives, the building's own back rooms, and an astronomical impossibility.
- **A connected storyline, not a set of gags.** Five traces of whoever came before you — a page, a photograph, a recording, a handprint, a name — assemble the answer. Two spaces stay sealed until you have earned your way in, and a gate always refuses in words rather than in silence.
- An unreliable narrator that begins as a pompous administrator and turns out to be something much sadder.
- Original writing (98 authored lines), geometry, procedural textures and synthesized ambient audio.
- Optional narration using an installed **local** English system voice — no cloud voice and no imitation of any actor. Captions carry the whole story regardless.
- Settings, discoveries and clues stay in your own browser.

## Controls

**WASD** walk · **Mouse** look · **E** interact · **Shift** hurry · **Esc** pause.
Pause holds the clue dossier, the full dialogue transcript, and honest exit controls.

## Limitations
